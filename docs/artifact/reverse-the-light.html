The frame we are working in

Positioning · one sentence each

The split

set.a.light 3D asks "what will this look like?" Build a setup in a physics room, render before you shoot. Deterministic, zero marginal cost, sells a perpetual license.

NGW asks "how was that done?" Upload a photograph, get the setup that made it. There is no forward simulation to fall back on — the read is the product, so the read has to be right, and it has to be checkable.

Marketing & promotion roadmap

Track 2 · starts at stage 2 · four phases, four exit conditions

Per doctrine this starts at stage 2, not after deploy — positioning shapes the product. The worked example is from this project: the confidence grade was removed because copy could not honestly say “every time,” and that changed what the result screen renders. Marketing found it, not engineering.

Each phase exits on a condition, not a date. The governing rule sits above all four — promotion readiness is gated by payment capability, not build state — so phase 3 blocks everything after it however finished the product looks.

Directions kept

Each one carries the measurement that earned it

D1

The engine analyzes. The AI supplements.

Physical CV — catchlights, shadow geometry, face landmarks — resolves the pattern. The VLM is a hint, never the answer, and never outranks strong physical evidence.

Ran the corpus with the VLM on and off: identical results on 34 of 34 — same pattern, same confidence, same resolver. It costs ~$0.0152 and ~10s per image and changes nothing.
D2

Evidence over verdict.

Show what the engine saw — catchlight at 10 o'clock, key 30–45° off-axis left, ~16–24″ softbox — not how sure it claims to be. A photographer can check an observation against the photo. They cannot check a percentage.

Reads labelled "Uncertain" were right 83% of the time; "Tentative" 100%. Correlation between score and correctness: +0.035. The grade was teaching people to ignore it.
D3

Publish the misses.

The accuracy gallery shows every reference read against human-verified truth, failures included, with the strict number next to the generous one. A proof surface that hides its failures is not a proof surface.

Reports exact and acceptable separately — 14 vs 26 of 29. Quoting only the second would be a lie of omission.
D4

Free is the product, not the trial.

The full CV read costs nothing to serve. Gating it behind a counter hides the one thing a buyer needs to audit before paying — whether it is actually right.

CV-only: 1.4–9.4s, median 4.6s (n=5, warm, re-measured 2026-09-02 — this page said 7.7s and HANDOFF said 0.7s, two numbers for one measurement with no record of how either was taken). No API spend, and the same accuracy as the paid path. Whatever the paid tier sells, it is not a better answer.
D5

Geometry first, semantics last.

Catchlight position before pattern. Pattern before setup family. Pattern before source context. Unknown direction is not on-axis. Semantic hints only after physical grounding.

Enforced as ordering, not preference. When a coordinate bug silenced the geometry passes, resolution fell through to other sources and nobody noticed for months.
D6

One vocabulary per question.

Shadow geometry, tonality, source context, and named setup are four different questions. One field cannot answer all four without lying about at least three.

Three pattern vocabularies exist today, overlapping on 5 of 28 values. The migration meant to separate them expired 2026-05-06 and never finished.

Directions struck

Considered, measured, rejected — kept here so they are not re-proposed

✕ S1

Paid tier = "Deep Read" with the VLM.

The ladder assumed the VLM bought a better answer, so the paid tier could sell depth. It does not buy a better answer.

Struck. Identical on 34/34. A tier that sells 10 extra seconds and an API bill is not a tier. The ladder needs a different value metric.
✕ S2

Fix the coordinate bug, ship the fix.

A face-detection box was emitted in the wrong coordinate space — provably wrong, negative width, red-proofed test. Fixing it alone made the product worse.

Struck. Corpus accuracy fell 15/34 → 5/34 exact. The downstream passes are calibrated against the broken values. It lands with its consumers retuned, or not at all.
✕ S3

Recalibrate confidence from the corpus.

Tempting: fit a mapping from stated confidence to measured accuracy and ship a calibrated number.

Struck. n=29. A map fitted to 29 samples is overfitting wearing a lab coat — the same error as S2, one layer up. It needs more labelled data, not more arithmetic.
