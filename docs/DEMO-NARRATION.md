# Narration script — NGW Lighting demo

**For:** `ngw-demo-1440x900.mp4` — 1:50, captioned desktop walkthrough.
**Voice:** calm, unhurried, low-register. A working photographer explaining a tool
to another working photographer — not a product launch.

**Pace target: 125 words per minute** — roughly 2 words per second.

The script is **167 words**, about **80 seconds** of speech inside a 110-second
video. The remaining 30 seconds are silence, and the silence is deliberate: the
shots at 0:40 and 0:52 carry themselves. Do not fill them.

Every line is written to fit the gap before the next cut, so the read never
falls behind picture. The `fit` column shows seconds available against seconds
needed.

---

## Delivery direction

- **Land every sentence downward.** No upward lift at the end of a line; that is
  the sound of selling, and it is the one thing that will cost credibility with
  this audience.
- **Short sentences, full stops.** Let the period do the work. Where the script
  has a line break, take a breath.
- **Slow into the uncertainty beat (0:40).** That passage should be the slowest
  in the read — it is the claim the whole product rests on.
- **No emphasis on numbers.** Say "thirty-one" plainly. Numbers oversold sound
  like numbers invented.
- **Nothing superlative.** There is no "powerful," "seamless," "revolutionary"
  in this script on purpose. Do not add them in the booth.

| **1:20** | Build from scratch | It runs the other way too. No photograph — describe the subject and the look, and it builds the setup. | 13s / 9s |
| **1:33** | Journal | Every read is kept — what you shot, and how it was lit. | 6s / 6s |

The two long gaps — **1:00** and **1:20** — are intentional. The recipe grid and
the setup builder are dense screens; let the viewer read them.

## If you need it shorter

Cut these three lines first, in this order. They carry the least and free about
about 18 seconds:

1. **1:33 — Journal.** The visual reads without narration.
2. **0:11 — "Start with any photograph."** The screen already says *Load photo*.
3. **0:20 — the crop line.** Keep it only if the audience is non-photographers.

**Never cut 0:40.** If the whole read has to collapse to two sentences, they are
the two at 0:40.

## What not to say

These are unsupported by the build in the video, and claiming them would not
survive a question:

- **Do not cite accuracy.** No benchmark figure is quoted here, and the corpus
  accuracy gate is deselected from a default test run.
- **Do not describe the analysis speed as representative.** The VLM did not run
  in this recording — no API key was present — so the analysis completes faster
  here than a full production read would. Full wall time is unmeasured.
- **Do not present it as live.** The closing card says "recorded walkthrough" for
  this reason.
- **Do not mention pricing tiers as purchasable.** Stripe is not activated; the
  upgrade panel visible on screen cannot currently take a payment.
